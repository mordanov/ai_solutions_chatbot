# Parking Facility — General Information

## About Us

Welcome to CityPark — a modern, secure indoor parking facility in the heart of the city.
We offer 24/7 access for registered users and provide a safe, well-lit environment for
your vehicle.

## Facilities and Services

- 350 total parking spaces across 5 floors
- Dedicated spaces for disabled drivers (floor 1, near entrance)
- EV charging stations available on floors 1 and 2 (12 stations total)
- CCTV surveillance on all floors and entrances
- On-site security personnel 07:00–23:00
- Automated barrier entry and exit with licence plate recognition
- Monthly and annual subscription plans available
