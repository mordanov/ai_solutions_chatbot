# Parking Facility — Location

## Address

**CityPark**
42 Market Street
City Centre
Postcode: CC1 2AB

## Getting Here

**By car**: Enter from Market Street. The barrier entrance is on the right side of the
building, clearly signposted. Follow the green LED arrows inside to available spaces.

**By public transport**: The nearest metro station is Central Square (lines 1 and 3),
a 3-minute walk. Bus stops 14 and 22 stop directly outside.

**On foot**: We are located 5 minutes from City Hall and 10 minutes from the main
shopping centre.
