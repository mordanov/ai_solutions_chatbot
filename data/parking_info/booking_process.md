# Parking Facility — Booking Process

## How to Reserve a Space

You can reserve a parking space in advance through our chatbot or website. Advance
reservations guarantee you a space for your chosen period.

### Steps

1. Start a conversation with the CityPark chatbot.
2. Tell the chatbot you would like to reserve a space.
3. Provide the following information when asked:
   - Your first name
   - Your surname
   - Your vehicle licence plate number
   - Your desired start date and time
   - Your desired end date and time
4. Review your reservation details when the chatbot summarises them.
5. Confirm the reservation. It will be sent to our team for approval.
6. You will receive a confirmation or rejection message once our team has reviewed it.

## Reservation Policies

- Reservations must be made at least 1 hour in advance.
- A reserved space is held for 15 minutes past the reservation start time. After that,
  the space may be released if you have not arrived.
- Cancellations made more than 2 hours before the start time are free of charge.
- Late cancellations (within 2 hours) may incur a 50% fee.

## Walk-In Parking

Walk-in spaces (no advance reservation) are available on a first-come, first-served
basis subject to availability. Drive in and the barrier will issue a ticket. Pay on exit.

## Payment

Payment is accepted at the payment machines on each floor and at the exit barriers.
We accept credit/debit cards and contactless payment. Cash is not accepted.
