# Parking Facility — Rules and Policies

## General Rules

1. All vehicles must be registered with a valid licence plate before entering.
2. Maximum vehicle height: 2.1 m. Vehicles exceeding this limit cannot enter.
3. Speed limit inside the facility: 10 km/h.
4. Drivers must follow floor markings and directional arrows at all times.
5. Parking outside designated bays is strictly prohibited and may result in clamping.
6. No overnight sleeping in vehicles.
7. No refuelling or vehicle repairs inside the facility.
8. Animals must remain in the vehicle; no pets in communal areas.

## Disabled Bays

Disabled bays are reserved exclusively for vehicles displaying a valid disabled badge.
Misuse will result in a penalty notice and possible clamping.

## EV Charging

EV charging bays are reserved for electric vehicles actively charging. Once charging is
complete, vehicles must be moved within 30 minutes to avoid a waiting fee.

## Lost Tickets / Licence Plate Issues

If the licence plate recognition fails on exit, proceed to the help booth on floor 1 or
use the intercom at the exit barrier. A member of staff will assist you.

## Liability

CityPark is not liable for theft of or damage to vehicles or their contents beyond what
is covered by our CCTV and security monitoring policies.
